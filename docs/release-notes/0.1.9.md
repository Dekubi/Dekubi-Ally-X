## Reviewed, not taken
- VIctory-Belobug-AMD: 110 changes past upstream 8e79c80; a full merge with this
  tree's base gave ~540 conflict hunks. Its main speed change (title memory used
  in place, OpenExistingHeapFromAddress1) exists here as DEKUBI_INPLACE; a run on
  the RTX 5060 with DEKUBI_INPLACE=1 put 364 MB in place and the card's time per
  frame went from ~130 to 370-520 ms. Its descriptor-slot and guard fixes remain
  candidates for later, one at a time.
- leonida-legacy, leonida-ubiquitarianism (GPL-3.0, C++/C#/Python, D3D12->Vulkan;
  their READMEs say nothing is playable yet) and Xbox2Windows (MIT, static
  recompilation research): different designs, nothing to port yet.
